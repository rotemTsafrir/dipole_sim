Interactive Antenna & Electromagnetic Field Simulator

AntennaSim is a free browser-based tool for exploring electromagnetic fields and antenna behavior. Place antenna elements, change their parameters, and visualize the resulting electric field, magnetic field, and energy flow in real time.

Experiment with center-fed dipoles, Hertzian dipoles, loops, phased arrays, and coupled passive conducting wires. Try built-in examples including a half-wave dipole, Yagi–Uda antenna, and electric quadrupole configuration.

This is an educational visualization tool that uses simplified models and numerical approximations; it is not a substitute for full 3D electromagnetic simulation or antenna measurements.
